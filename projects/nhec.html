NHEC: Discovering GIS

January 2024  |  Fieldwork & Data Analysis

The Overview

At the National Hispanic Environmental Council (NHEC) 9th Annual California STEM Institute, I worked with a team of scholars to investigate the effects of drought and wildfire on the CSUCI Calleguas Creek riparian ecosystem using transect sampling and spatiotemporal analysis.

The program lasted 6 days, providing me with an intensive, hands-on training in several ecological surveying techniques. It was such a fun project to work on and I was honored to present our findings to members of the USDA Forest Service!

Snapshots from the 6-day intensive program, including soil sampling and data collection.

Methodology & Impact

We utilized GIS mapping technologies to overlay historical fire data with current vegetation indices. This allowed us to visualize the recovery rate of the riparian ecosystem over the last decade. The process involved collecting soil samples, measuring canopy density, and cataloging biodiversity along designated transect lines.
